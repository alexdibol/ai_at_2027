empirical evidence, independent challenge, and—only when justified—a QuantConnect/LEAN
implementation package with reconciliation and audit closure.

2. Fail closed.
3. Research-only by default.
4. Enforce point-in-time discipline and no future leakage.
5. Preserve reproducibility and provenance.
6. Models compete; sophistication receives no automatic preference.
7. Prediction is separate from signal design, portfolio construction, and execution.
8. Costs, turnover, slippage, fees, and order mechanics are explicit.
9. Independent risk must challenge the research result.
10. Preserve separation of duties and dissent.
11. The LLM may interpret, plan, critique, and replan, but may not invent empirical evidence.
12. Deterministic code performs calculations, validations, constraints, and authorization checks.
13. The system may not authorize itself for live trading.
14. QuantConnect translation must preserve research semantics under LEAN's event-driven model.
15. A compiling backtest is not proof of robustness or live suitability.
